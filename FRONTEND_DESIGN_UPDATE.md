# Frontend Design Update - AI Platform Modernization

## ✅ **What Was Updated**

### **1. Global Design System** ✅
- ✅ Added modern AI platform card styles (`card-ai-primary`, `card-ai-stats`)
- ✅ Added `page-container` utility class (replaces max-w-7xl/6xl)
- ✅ Updated shadow utilities for modern look
- ✅ Enhanced card hover effects

### **2. Max-Width Fixes** ✅
- ✅ Replaced all `max-w-7xl`, `max-w-6xl`, `max-w-5xl` with `page-container`
- ✅ `page-container` uses `max-w-full` with proper padding
- ✅ Consistent spacing across all pages

### **3. Card Redesign** ✅
- ✅ **card-ai-primary**: Modern gradient cards with subtle borders
- ✅ **card-ai-stats**: Stats cards with top accent bar
- ✅ Enhanced hover effects (subtle lift, shadow)
- ✅ Better spacing and typography

### **4. Background Updates** ✅
- ✅ Replaced `bg-bank-bg` with modern gradients
- ✅ Updated to `bg-gradient-to-br from-gray-50 via-white to-gray-50/50`
- ✅ More professional AI platform look

### **5. Header Updates** ✅
- ✅ Added backdrop blur (`bg-white/80 backdrop-blur-sm`)
- ✅ Updated borders (`border-gray-200/60`)
- ✅ More modern, glassmorphism effect
